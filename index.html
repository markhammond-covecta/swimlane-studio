Swimlane syntax


      
Messages

      

        
A -> B: msgForward message. The activity box sits in lane A; the arrow lands on lane B.

        
A --> B: msgForward message, drawn dashed (typically a response).

        
A <- B: msgReversed: B sends to A. Equivalent to B -> A: msg.

        
A <-> B: msgBidirectional same-lane link. Adds a forward trail to the next same-lane box and a backward trail from the previous same-lane box.

        
B >-> A: msgForce the shared-column counter-pair rendering when responding to the immediately previous message (otherwise the chain breaks and B's reply goes in a new column).

        
A <: textBackward-only box: a labelled box in lane A with just an incoming trail from the previous same-lane box.

        
A: textStandalone box: a labelled box in lane A with no arrows of its own. A preceding cross-lane arrow will still land on it.

        
A -> A: textSame-lane forward link. The box sits in lane A and a horizontal arrow trails to the next same-lane box.

        
[1] A -> B: textTag the box this line creates as 1 (any name works). The tag is never drawn; it just gives a later statement a loop-back target.

        
C -> [1]: textLoop back to the box tagged 1: a box in lane C with a backward arc to the tagged box. A same-lane loop stays within its lane; a cross-lane loop routes through a channel above the lanes. The arc never crosses a box, and breaks where it crosses another connector.

      


      
Notes

      

        
note A: textYellow note in lane A.

        
note A, B: textNote spanning lanes A and B (and any lanes in between).

      


      
Layout

      

        
title: ...Diagram title, rendered above the lanes.

        
order: A, B, CExplicit lane order (top to bottom). Any lanes referenced but not listed are appended in first-seen order.

        
[ Section ]Banner spanning the columns of the following events, up to the next section or end of diagram.

      


      
Captions and labels

      

        
caption \n moreLine break inside a box caption or arrow label. /n works too.

        
caption <arrow label>Trailing <...> becomes the arrow's italic side-label; the rest stays in the box.

      


      
Comments

      

        
# commentLine comment.

        
// commentLine comment.

      


      
Editing

      

        
Click a shapeSelects its line in the source; moving the caret onto a line highlights the matching shapes.

        
Double-click a lane nameRename the lane inline. The new name replaces every reference to that lane in the source. Enter to apply, Esc to cancel.

        
Double-click a box, note, or sectionEdit its text inline. Enter to apply, Shift+Enter for a line break, Esc to cancel.

        
Double-click the titleEdit the diagram title inline. Enter to apply, Esc to cancel.

        
Undo / RedoStep backwards or forwards through source edits with the buttons or Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z.

        
Lane autocompleteTyping a lane name (line start, after an arrow, or in a note/order list) suggests existing lanes in the current diagram. ↑/↓ to move, Tab to accept, Esc to dismiss.
